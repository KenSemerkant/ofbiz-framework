## Deviations (flagged, not silently averaged)
1. **Topic spelling:** topics are `ofbiz.<TABLE>` (ofbiz.PARTY, ofbiz.PERSON,
   ofbiz.PARTY_GROUP, ofbiz.PARTY_ROLE); the `<action>` rides the Debezium
   envelope `op` field (c/u/d/r) instead of the topic suffix. Consumers map
   op -> created/updated/deleted (ADR-012 template unchanged).
2. **Value format:** JSON with embedded schema, not Avro. KNOWN ISSUE: the
   Debezium image's Apicurio AvroConverter loads (plugin dir fix) but its
   serde stays in artifact-lookup mode — the `apicurio.registry.auto-register`
   key contains a dash, which the image's CONNECT_* env mapping cannot
   express, and connector-level converter overrides do not reach worker
   source-task converters. Avro conversion deferred; service-owned topics
   stay Avro per ADR-009.
3. **Local transport:** plaintext internal listener 9092 (Strimzi KafkaConnect
   CR is incompatible with the stock Debezium image layout); the cdc-connect
   KafkaUser manifest is the cloud-profile mTLS identity.
